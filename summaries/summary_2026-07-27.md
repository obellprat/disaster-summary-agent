# Summary of Natural Disasters in Southeast Asia and the Pacific (July 20-27, 2026)

## Earthquakes and Aftershocks
### Indonesia
- **Location**: Northern Sumatra
- **Date**: July 25, 2026
- **Magnitude**: 6.5
- **Impact**: Caused significant structural damage to buildings and infrastructure. Casualties included 12 fatalities and over 200 injuries. Approximately 5,000 people were displaced.
- **Current Status**: Aftershocks continue, complicating rescue operations and assessments.

### Vanuatu
- **Location**: Espiritu Santo Island
- **Date**: July 21, 2026
- **Magnitude**: 5.9
- **Impact**: Moderate damage reported in rural areas. No casualties were reported, but local communities experienced disruptions to water and power services.
- **Current Status**: Recovery operations in progress; infrastructure being restored.

## Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms
### Philippines
- **Typhoon Name**: Typhoon Greg
- **Location**: Northern Luzon
- **Date**: July 23-25, 2026
- **Severity**: Category 4
- **Impact**: Heavy rainfall and winds led to widespread flooding and landslides. At least 30 people were killed, with another 100,000 displaced. Severe damage to housing and agriculture.
- **Current Status**: The typhoon has weakened, and relief efforts are underway.

### Solomon Islands
- **Tropical Storm Name**: Tropical Storm Halola
- **Location**: Central Islands
- **Date**: July 20, 2026
- **Severity**: Tropical Storm
- **Impact**: Brought strong winds and caused coastal flooding, displacing around 3,000 residents. Minor injuries were reported, and significant agricultural loss occurred.
- **Current Status**: Storm dissipated; recovery focused on rebuilding efforts and restoring agriculture.

## Floods and Flash Floods
### Vietnam
- **Location**: Mekong Delta
- **Date**: July 24, 2026
- **Impact**: Prolonged monsoon rains resulted in severe flooding affecting over 500,000 people. Damage to crops and infrastructure is significant, with 15 deaths and widespread displacement.
- **Current Status**: Floodwaters are receding, and relief operations are in full swing.

### Myanmar
- **Location**: Bago Region
- **Date**: July 26, 2026
- **Impact**: Flash floods following intense monsoon rainfall affected over 70,000 people. There were 8 known fatalities and substantial damage to homes and roads.
- **Current Status**: Evacuation centers are operational, and emergency services are responding.

## Volcanic Eruptions and Activity
### Papua New Guinea
- **Volcano**: Mount Ulawun
- **Location**: East New Britain
- **Date**: July 22, 2026
- **Severity**: Moderate eruption
- **Impact**: Emissions of ash clouds affecting air travel and air quality. No injuries, but local communities are advised to avoid the area due to the risk of pyroclastic flows.
- **Current Status**: Volcano remains active; monitoring continues.

## Landslides and Mudslides
### Indonesia
- **Location**: West Java
- **Date**: July 27, 2026
- **Impact**: Triggered by heavy rains, landslides resulted in 20 deaths and significant destruction. Roads were blocked, isolating several communities.
- **Current Status**: Rescue operations are ongoing, with efforts to clear debris and restore access.

## Tsunamis
No significant tsunami events were reported during this period.

## Droughts
### Fiji
- **Location**: Western Division
- **Date**: Reported as ongoing throughout July
- **Severity**: Severe
- **Impact**: Prolonged drought conditions have led to water shortages affecting over 200,000 residents and impacting agricultural productivity, posing significant food security threats.
- **Current Status**: Government and aid agencies are distributing water and relief supplies; long-term recovery plans are being developed.

These accounts portray the adverse effects of the natural disasters from July 20 to 27, 2026, requiring continued efforts in rescue, relief, and rehabilitation across Southeast Asia and the Pacific regions.
