### **FLOODS AND FLASH FLOODS**

**Indonesia - Malinau Regency Flooding**
- **Type:** Flooding
- **Location:** Malinau Regency, North Kalimantan, Indonesia
- **Date:** Ongoing during March 16-22, 2026 (continuing into the period)
- **Severity:** Major flooding following persistent heavy rainfall causing rivers to overflow
- **Impact:** 
Approximately 5,000 families (about 43,000 people) affected across 13 villages in five sub-districts. Around 5,000 houses impacted, at least 23 education facilities and government offices damaged, disrupting local services and livelihoods

- **Current Status:** 
Floodwaters have receded and residents have returned to normal activities

- **Source:** AHA Centre Weekly Disaster Update, March 16-22, 2026

### **VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY**

**Philippines - Bulusan Volcano**
- **Type:** Volcanic unrest
- **Location:** Bulusan Volcano, Sorsogon Province, Philippines
- **Date:** Alert level raised on March 22, 2026
- **Severity:** 
Alert level raised from 0 to 1, volcano entered a period of low-level unrest

- **Impact:** 
475 volcanic earthquakes recorded since March 15
. 
Ground deformation data indicate inflation or swelling of the western and southeastern flank since the last week of January and first week of March 2026

- **Current Status:** 
Increased likelihood of sudden steam-driven eruptions. Entry into the 4-kilometer radius Permanent Danger Zone strictly prohibited

- **Source:** Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS), March 22, 2026

**Philippines - Kanlaon Volcano**
- **Type:** Volcanic eruption (ongoing)
- **Location:** Kanlaon Volcano, Negros Island Region, Philippines
- **Date:** Continuing eruption through March 2026
- **Severity:** 
Alert Level 2 (Moderate Level of Volcanic Unrest). During March 18-25, the seismic network recorded 5-18 daily volcanic earthquakes, though on March 23 and 24 there were 67 and 63 volcanic earthquakes, respectively

- **Impact:** 
Plumes rose to a maximum of 150 m above the vent on March 10, 2026

- **Current Status:** Eruption continuing with moderate unrest
- **Source:** PHIVOLCS Weekly Reports, March 2026

**Philippines - Mayon Volcano**
- **Type:** Volcanic eruption (ongoing)
- **Location:** Mayon Volcano, Albay Province, Philippines  
- **Date:** Continuing eruption through March 18-25, 2026
- **Severity:** Alert Level 3
- **Impact:** 
Eruption characterized by lava effusion and collapses at the summit dome, pyroclastic density currents (PDCs), incandescent rockfalls, ash plumes, active lava flows, and occasional minor Strombolian activity

- **Current Status:** Active eruption ongoing
- **Source:** PHIVOLCS, March 18-25, 2026

**Indonesia - Semeru Volcano**
- **Type:** Volcanic eruption
- **Location:** Semeru Volcano, East Java, Indonesia
- **Date:** March 29, 2026
- **Severity:** 
Explosive eruption at 08:49 LT producing an ash plume rising 800 m above the summit and a pyroclastic density current traveling 3.5 km toward the Besuk Kobokan drainage. Eruption lasted approximately 4 minutes and 6 seconds with a maximum seismic amplitude of 22 mm

- **Impact:** 
Alert Level 3 maintained. Exclusion zone of 5 km from the summit in effect, with extended restrictions up to 13 km along the southeast sector following the Besuk Kobokan drainage

- **Current Status:** Active, continued monitoring
- **Source:** Indonesia's PVMBG, March 29, 2026

**Indonesia - Mount Marapi, Lewotobi Laki-laki, and Ibu**
- **Type:** Volcanic activity
- **Location:** Multiple volcanoes in Indonesia
- **Date:** March 16-22, 2026
- **Severity:**

- **Impact:** Ongoing monitoring, no major incidents reported
