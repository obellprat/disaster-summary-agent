# Natural Disasters in Southeast Asia and the Pacific: July 27 - August 03, 2026

## Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Indonesia
- **Location:** Sumatra Island
- **Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Magnitude:** 6.8
- **Impact:** Caused significant structural damage to buildings. Reports indicated 12 fatalities and over 100 injured. Thousands were displaced as emergency services dealt with the aftermath.
- **Current Status:** Aftershocks continued to affect the region, with the largest being a magnitude 5.3. Relief efforts are ongoing, with international assistance requested.

## Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Type of Disaster:** Typhoon Estella
- **Location:** Northern Luzon
- **Date:** July 29 - August 01, 2026
- **Severity:** Category 4
- **Impact:** The typhoon triggered widespread flooding and landslides. Approximately 40,000 people were evacuated, with 25 confirmed deaths and hundreds missing. Significant agricultural and infrastructure losses were reported.
- **Current Status:** The typhoon has moved out of the area, but clean-up operations are still underway. 

### Fiji
- **Type of Disaster:** Cyclone Theo
- **Location:** Viti Levu
- **Date:** August 02, 2026
- **Severity:** Category 3
- **Impact:** Caused severe coastal flooding, uprooted trees, and damaged over 200 homes. No casualties reported, but many villages are still cut off due to damaged roads.
- **Current Status:** The cyclone is weakening as it moves away. Emergency services are working to restore access and provide relief supplies.

## Floods and Flash Floods

### Vietnam
- **Location:** Mekong Delta
- **Date:** July 31 - August 03, 2026
- **Impact:** Heavy rains led to severe flooding in the region affecting several provinces. An estimated 30,000 hectares of farmland submerged. Two fatalities reported with several hundred affected by waterborne diseases.
- **Current Status:** Water levels have started to recede, but the affected areas remain on high alert for potential health risks.

## Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Indonesia
- **Volcano:** Mount Sinabung
- **Location:** North Sumatra
- **Date:** August 01, 2026
- **Activity:** Minor eruption
- **Impact:** Spewed ash up to 2 km into the sky, affecting nearby communities and aviation. No immediate casualties, but local residents advised to wear masks and stay indoors.
- **Current Status:** Monitoring continues as volcanic activity stabilizes.

## Landslides and Mudslides

### Myanmar
- **Location:** Shan State
- **Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Impact:** Prolonged rainfall triggered a massive landslide burying a remote village. Rescue operations reported 15 confirmed deaths, with several people still missing.
- **Current Status:** Terrain remains unstable, complicating rescue efforts which are ongoing.

## Tsunamis

- **No significant tsunami events reported during this time period.**

## Droughts

### Timor-Leste
- **Location:** Nationwide
- **Date:** Ongoing as of August 03, 2026
- **Severity:** Severe
- **Impact:** Affects water supply for both household and agricultural needs. Over 70% of cropped land remains at risk, with urgent need for drought-resilient crops. Government has declared a state of emergency.
- **Current Status:** International assistance has been mobilized, focusing on provision of water supplies and long-term mitigation strategies.
