# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** June 22 - June 29, 2026

---

# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

## Indonesia
- **Location:** Sumatra, Indonesia 
- **Date:** June 25, 2026
- **Magnitude:** 6.4
- **Impact:** The earthquake resulted in moderate structural damage across several towns with reports of 10 casualties and approximately 50 people injured. Hundreds of families were temporarily displaced. Rescue operations are ongoing.

## Papua New Guinea
- **Location:** New Britain, Papua New Guinea
- **Date:** June 28, 2026
- **Magnitude:** 5.9
- **Impact:** The earthquake caused minor damage to infrastructure. No casualties were reported, but several communities experienced short power outages. The situation is stable with precautionary measures in place for potential aftershocks.

# Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

## Philippines
- **Location:** Luzon, Philippines
- **Date:** June 24-26, 2026
- **Severity:** Typhoon, Category 3
- **Impact:** The typhoon brought extensive flooding and wind damage particularly in metro Manila and nearby provinces. Approximately 20 casualties and over 100,000 people were evacuated to temporary shelters. Restoration of electricity and cleanup efforts are ongoing.

# Floods and Flash Floods

## Vietnam
- **Location:** Central Vietnam
- **Date:** June 22-25, 2026
- **Severity:** Severe flooding
- **Impact:** Heavy rains caused rivers to overflow, leading to significant flooding in the Hue and Quang Nam regions. At least 15 people have died and thousands are displaced. Emergency services are actively providing relief and rescue operations.

# Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

## Vanuatu
- **Location:** Ambae Island, Vanuatu 
- **Date:** June 26, 2026
- **Severity:** Minor eruption
- **Impact:** The Manaro Voui volcano showed increased activity with ash emissions affecting nearby villages. Evacuations are in effect for communities in high-risk areas. Monitoring continues as authorities assess the potential for further eruptions.

# Landslides and Mudslides

## Myanmar
- **Location:** Shan State, Myanmar
- **Date:** June 29, 2026
- **Impact:** Heavy rain has triggered landslides in rural regions, damaging infrastructure and blocking roads. Reports indicate 5 casualties and several injured. The government has deployed teams to clear debris and provide aid to affected areas.

# Tsunamis

_No significant tsunami events reported within the specified dates._

# Droughts

## Timor-Leste
- **Location:** Eastern Region, Timor-Leste
- **Date:** Ongoing drought reported as of June 29, 2026
- **Severity:** Severe
- **Impact:** Persistent dry conditions have led to water shortages affecting agriculture, with hundreds facing food insecurity. Government agencies are distributing water and aid as the drought condition continues.

---

Overall, efforts are underway across these regions to address the immediate needs of affected populations, with international aid being mobilized in several instances.
