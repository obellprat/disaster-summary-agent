# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** September 07 - September 14, 2026

---

## Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Indonesia
- **Location**: Sulawesi
- **Date**: September 9, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude**: 6.3 magnitude
- **Impact**: Several buildings damaged, 3 fatalities, 50 injured, and hundreds displaced; infrastructure repairs underway.
- **Current Status**: Emergency services engaged in rescue operations and assessment of damage.

## Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Type of Disaster**: Typhoon
- **Location**: Luzon and Visayas regions
- **Date**: September 11-13, 2026
- **Severity**: Category 4
- **Impact**: 20,000 displaced, 12 fatalities, widespread flooding and power outages; significant agricultural damage.
- **Current Status**: Relief operations ongoing, power restoration efforts in process.

### Vietnam
- **Type of Disaster**: Tropical Storm
- **Location**: Central Vietnam
- **Date**: September 8-10, 2026
- **Severity**: Strong tropical storm
- **Impact**: Minimal casualties but thousands affected, local flooding, several homes and roads damaged.
- **Current Status**: Cleanup and recovery in affected areas.

### Solomon Islands
- **Type of Disaster**: Tropical Cyclone
- **Location**: Throughout the islands
- **Date**: September 10-12, 2026
- **Severity**: Category 1
- **Impact**: Minor structural damage, no fatalities reported, communications disrupted for a few days.
- **Current Status**: Situation stabilizing with ongoing repairs to infrastructure.

## Floods and Flash Floods

### Myanmar
- **Location**: Southern Regions
- **Date**: September 12-14, 2026
- **Impact**: Severe flooding displaced over 15,000 people, caused major transportation disruptions, and led to 8 fatalities.
- **Current Status**: Floodwaters are receding, with ongoing efforts to provide aid and shelter to displaced communities.

## Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Indonesia
- **Location**: Mount Merapi
- **Date**: September 14, 2026
- **Severity**: Moderate eruption
- **Impact**: Ash clouds disrupted flights, evacuation orders issued for nearby villages, no immediate casualties reported.
- **Current Status**: Volcanic activity being closely monitored, affected population advised to stay alert.

## Landslides and Mudslides

### Papua New Guinea
- **Location**: Highlands Region
- **Date**: September 13, 2026
- **Impact**: Triggered by heavy rains, landslide blocked roads, damaged homes, 5 fatalities reported, over 200 people displaced.
- **Current Status**: Rescue efforts underway, clearing of roads being prioritized.

## Tsunamis

- No significant tsunami events reported in the specified regions during this period.

## Droughts

### Timor-Leste
- **Severity**: Severe drought
- **Impact**: Significant impact on agriculture, threatening food security for rural communities, water supply issues.
- **Current Status**: Government and NGOs working on water management and distribution of relief supplies to affected areas.
