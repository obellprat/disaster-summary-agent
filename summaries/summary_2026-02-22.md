## TROPICAL STORMS AND CYCLONES

### Tropical Storm Penha (Basyang)
**Type:** Tropical Storm  
**Location:** Philippines (Mindanao and Visayas regions)  
**Date:** 
February 5-7, 2026
  
**Severity:** Moderate tropical storm with multiple landfalls  
**Impact:** 
- 
Deaths: 12 fatalities (4 in Cagayan de Oro, 5 in Iligan, 2 in Surigao del Sur, 1 in Carmen, Agusan del Norte)

- 
36 injuries in Northern Mindanao

- 
1,373 houses damaged

- 
Economic loss in Surigao del Sur: ₱1.48 billion (US$25.24 million)

- 
State of calamity declared in Iligan City and Surigao del Sur


**Current Status:** 
Storm weakened into a low-pressure area on February 7

## VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY

### Kanlaon Volcano - Philippines

**Type:** Volcanic eruption  
**Location:** Negros Island Region, Philippines  
**Date:** 
February 19, 2026 at 4:38 PM
  
**Severity:** 
Moderately explosive eruption lasting two minutes
  
**Magnitude:** 
Eruption plume rose to 2,000 m (6,600 ft) and drifted southwest
  
**Impact:** 
- 
Pyroclastic flows descended southern and southwestern slopes

- 
Sulfurous fumes observed in barangays of La Carlota City, Bago City, and Moises Padilla

- 
Local governments ordered to enforce strict safety precautions and keep danger zones clear

- No casualties reported from this specific eruption

**Current Status:** 
Remains under Alert Level 2 as of February 20, with continued signs of unrest
  
**Source:** PHIVOLCS, The Manila Times, Wikipedia

### Mount Semeru - Indonesia
