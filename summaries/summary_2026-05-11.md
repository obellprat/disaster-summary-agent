## VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY

### Indonesia - Mount Dukono (Halmahera Island)
**Date:** 
May 8, 2026

**Location:** 
Halmahera island, North Maluku

**Type:** 
Explosive eruption with thick ash column

**Severity:** 
Ash cloud reached 10 km (6 miles) into the sky

**Casualties:** 
3 people killed (two Singaporeans and one Indonesian), 5 injured

**Context:** 
About 20 climbers were on the volcano when it erupted at 7:41 AM local time, defying safety restrictions

**Evacuations:** 
14 climbers including 7 foreign nationals safely evacuated; 5 evacuated climbers reported injured

**Current Status:** 
Ongoing rumbling, search-and-rescue operations completed on May 10, 2026
. 
The volcano erupted again several times on May 9, once spewing an ash column about 3 kilometers high

**Alert Level:** 
Second highest alert level

**Restrictions:** 
4 km exclusion zone around the crater imposed since December 2024

**Source:** Indonesian authorities (BNPB, BMKG), NBC News, Gulf Times
