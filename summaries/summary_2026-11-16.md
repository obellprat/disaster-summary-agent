# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** November 09 - November 16, 2026

---

# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Papua New Guinea
- **Location**: Bougainville, Papua New Guinea
- **Date**: November 11, 2026
- **Magnitude**: 6.5
- **Impact**: Minimal infrastructure damage reported; no casualties or significant injuries. Some minor landslides occurred in the region.
- **Current Status**: Aftershocks continue, but no significant damage reported.

# Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Type**: Typhoon
- **Location**: Northern Luzon, Philippines
- **Date**: November 10, 2026
- **Severity**: Category 4
- **Impact**: Over 200,000 people displaced. Severe wind and flood damage in coastal areas. 12 casualties reported.
- **Current Status**: Relief operations ongoing, with aid distribution and infrastructure repair in progress.

### Vietnam
- **Type**: Tropical Storm
- **Location**: Central Vietnam
- **Date**: November 12, 2026
- **Severity**: Tropical Storm
- **Impact**: Widespread flooding, particularly in Da Nang; 50,000 displaced and 8 casualties reported. Extensive agricultural damage.
- **Current Status**: Storm dissipated; recovery efforts underway.

# Floods and Flash Floods

### Indonesia
- **Location**: Java, Indonesia
- **Date**: November 13, 2026
- **Severity**: Severe flooding
- **Impact**: Significant overflow of rivers causing evacuations of over 100,000 individuals. Property damages substantial in several cities including Jakarta.
- **Current Status**: Water levels decreasing; government providing food and shelters.

# Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Vanuatu
- **Location**: Mount Yasur, Tanna Island, Vanuatu
- **Date**: November 15, 2026
- **Severity**: Moderate eruption
- **Impact**: Ash fall affected local agriculture, minor injuries due to volcanic rocks. Evacuation of nearby villages.
- **Current Status**: Ongoing monitoring; volcanic activity stabilizing.

# Landslides and Mudslides

### Myanmar
- **Location**: Chin State, Myanmar
- **Date**: November 16, 2026
- **Severity**: Major mudslide
- **Impact**: Occurred following heavy rains, resulting in 15 casualties and burying several homes.
- **Current Status**: Rescue operations underway.

### Thailand
- **Location**: Northern Thailand
- **Date**: November 14, 2026
- **Severity**: Landslide
- **Impact**: Triggered by intense rains, causing the closure of several roads and minor injuries. No fatalities reported.
- **Current Status**: Road clearing operations in progress.

# Tsunamis

### Solomon Islands
- **Location**: South coast, Solomon Islands
- **Date**: November 11, 2026
- **Impact**: Triggered by the 6.5 earthquake near Papua New Guinea, minor tsunami waves observed, no significant damage or casualties.
- **Current Status**: Tsunami warnings lifted, normalcy restored.

# Droughts

### Timor-Leste
- **Location**: Nationwide, Timor-Leste
- **Date**: Continuing shortage as of November 2026
- **Severity**: Severe
- **Impact**: Prolonged drought affecting agriculture and water supply, impacting over 300,000 people.
- **Current Status**: Government and international agencies providing water relief and assistance.

This summary covers the natural disasters reported across the specified nations during the period from November 09 to November 16, 2026. The ongoing impacts are being mitigated by local governments and international aid organizations.
