## **VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY**

### Philippines - Mayon Volcano Eruption
**Location:** Albay Province, Legazpi City
**Date:** Ongoing activity; significant eruption on May 2, 2026 (approximately 5:30 PM)
**Severity:** Alert Level 3 (magmatic eruption in progress)
**Impact:** 
- 
Lava flows reached 3.8 kilometers down the mountain, particularly in the Basud gully

- 
Eruption kicked up a thick ash plume, visible from Legazpi City about 500km southeast of Manila

- Mass evacuations ordered within 6-kilometer danger zone
- 
Escalated lava flow effusion, ash plumes, and seismic activity

- Risk of pyroclastic density currents (PDCs) and ashfall affecting surrounding regions
**Current Status:** 
Alert Level 3 remains in effect; volcano under close monitoring

**Source:** The Asian Affairs, Gulf News, Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology
