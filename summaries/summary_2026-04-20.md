### **EARTHQUAKES AND AFTERSHOCKS**

**Major Earthquake - Indonesia (North Maluku/Molucca Sea)**
- **Type:** Major earthquake with tsunami
- **Location:** Molucca Sea, between North Sulawesi and North Maluku provinces, Indonesia (near Batang Dua Islands)
- **Date:** April 2, 2026 (with continuing aftershocks into April 13-20 period)
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Magnitude 7.3-7.4

- **Impact:** 
One person died, four were injured, and over 1,966 people were displaced. Additionally, 104 homes collapsed, 85 were moderately damaged, and 79 were slightly damaged in the Batang Dua Islands
. 
A 75 cm tsunami was observed in North Minahasa Regency, with smaller waves recorded in other locations

- **Current Status:** Aftershock sequence ongoing through mid-April. 
At least 1,378 aftershocks were recorded, with the largest having a magnitude of 6.2

- **Source:** BMKG, USGS, AHA Centre

### **TYPHOONS, CYCLONES, AND TROPICAL STORMS**

**Super Typhoon Sinlaku - Northern Mariana Islands (U.S. Territory, Western Pacific)**
*Note: While the Northern Mariana Islands are not part of Southeast Asia proper, this storm was monitored by Philippine agencies*

- **Type:** Super Typhoon (Category 5 equivalent)
- **Location:** 
Northern Mariana Islands (Saipan and Tinian)

- **Date:** 
Made landfall April 14, 2026

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Sustained winds of 150 mph with recorded gusts reaching 185 mph

- **Impact:** 
Widespread power blackouts and extensive damage to homes and infrastructure, with over 20 inches of rain causing heavy flooding
. 
No deaths reported so far. Power and water were out and many roads were impassable across Saipan and Tinian

- **Current Status:** 
Storm moved away from the islands on April 14-15, expected to curve toward sparsely populated volcanic islands in the far northern Marianas

- **Source:** NASA, NOAA, National Weather Service, PAGASA

### **VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY**

**Multiple Active Volcanoes - Philippines**

**Mount Kanlaon - Philippines**
- **Type:** Continuing volcanic eruption with explosive events
- **Location:** Negros Island, Philippines
- **Date:** Ongoing activity during April 8-14, 2026 period, with 
an explosive eruption on April 8 at 5:51 a.m.

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Produced a 4,000 metres (13,000 feet) high "voluminous" plume that drifted southwest along with pyroclastic flows

- **Impact:** 
On April 12-13, there were 150 and 102 volcanic earthquakes respectively
. 
Debris ejected by the eruption also ignited vegetation and caused wildfires near the crater

- **Current Status:** 
Alert Level remained at 2 (on a scale of 0-5)

- **Source:** PHIVOLCS

**Mount Mayon - Philippines**
- **Type:** Continuing volcanic eruption
- **Location:** Albay, Philippines  
- **Date:** Ongoing during April 8-14, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Characterized by lava effusion, pyroclastic density currents, incandescent rockfalls, ash plumes, and occasional minor Strombolian activity. Lava flows remained 1.3-3.8 km long

- **Impact:** 
By April 14, a total of 4,020 people (1,105 families) were staying in 12 evacuation shelters

- **Current Status:** 
Alert Level remained at 3 (on a 0-5 scale)

### **LANDSLIDES AND MUDSLIDES**

**Hailstorm-Related Damage - Vietnam**
- **Type:** Severe hailstorm causing structural damage (not traditional landslide but related geological hazard)
- **Location:** 
Bắc Hà Commune, Lào Cai Province, Vietnam

- **Date:** 
Early hours of April 16, 2026 (began at around 01:20-01:30 local time)

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Hailstones described as large as a fist or even a bowl fell alongside strong wind gusts

- **Impact:** 
Damaged 338 homes, injured four people, and affected 485.5 ha (1,200 acres) of crops. Economic losses exceeded VND 30 billion (USD 1.1-1.2 million)

- **Current Status:** Recovery ongoing
- **Source:** Lào Cai provincial government, GDACS, ADINet

**Ongoing Landslide Risks - Vietnam**
- **Location:** 
Lao Cai province, with thousands of landslide points on roads and highways, particularly in highland areas, during mid-April 2026

- **Impact:** 
As of April 16, four people were injured in Bác Hà commune, Lao Cai Province, with 105 people affected. Additionally, 21 houses were damaged, with three completely destroyed in Lam Dong and Lao Cai

- **Source:** GDACS, ADINet, Vietnam media
