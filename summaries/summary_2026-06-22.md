# Southeast Asia and Pacific Natural Disasters (June 15 - June 22, 2026)

## Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Indonesia
- **Location**: Central Sulawesi
- **Date**: June 19, 2026
- **Magnitude**: 6.4
- **Impact**: 5 casualties, approximately 50 injuries, major structural damage to buildings and infrastructure, with about 3000 people displaced.
- **Current Status**: Ongoing relief efforts, with focus on providing shelter and medical aid.

## Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Location**: Luzon Island
- **Date**: June 20-22, 2026
- **Severity**: Typhoon (Category 4) named Typhoon Domeng
- **Impact**: Over 1 million people affected, 20,000 evacuated, widespread flooding and power outages, with 10 reported fatalities.
- **Current Status**: Government agencies are conducting rescue operations and providing urgent support to affected communities.

## Floods and Flash Floods

### Vietnam
- **Location**: Central Vietnam
- **Date**: June 17-19, 2026
- **Severity**: Severe floods due to continuous heavy rainfall
- **Impact**: 8 deaths, over 50,000 people displaced, significant agricultural and infrastructural damage.
- **Current Status**: Floodwaters receding, teams focused on recovery and repair work, with urgent need for clean water and supplies.

### Malaysia
- **Location**: Eastern Malaysia (Sabah)
- **Date**: June 15-16, 2026
- **Severity**: Flash floods
- **Impact**: 2 fatalities, displacement of approximately 10,000 individuals, numerous road closures.
- **Current Status**: Flash floods subsided, clean-up operations underway.

## Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Papua New Guinea
- **Location**: Mount Ulawun
- **Date**: June 18, 2026
- **Severity**: Moderate eruption
- **Impact**: Ash plume affected nearby communities, displacing around 2,000 residents temporarily, minor disruptions to air travel.
- **Current Status**: Monitoring of volcanic activity continues, evacuation measures in place for potentially high-risk areas.

## Landslides and Mudslides

### Myanmar
- **Location**: Chin State
- **Date**: June 21, 2026
- **Severity**: Landslides following heavy rains
- **Impact**: 5 confirmed fatalities, several injuries, destruction of isolated rural roads and homes.
- **Current Status**: Search and rescue operations in progress, efforts to restore access to affected areas.
