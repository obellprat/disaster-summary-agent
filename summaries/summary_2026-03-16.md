**Location:** Luzon Island, Philippines  
**Date:** Eruption ongoing since January 6, 2026; continuing through March 9-16, 2026  
**Severity:** 
Alert level "3 - Increased Tendency Towards Hazardous Eruption" (on a 0–5 level scale)
  
**Impact:** 
Continued lava effusion feeding flows along three gullies on the volcano's southern and eastern slopes. Lava flows remained steady at 3.8 kilometers along the Basud gully, 2.9 kilometers along the Bonga gully, and 1.3 kilometers along the Mi-isi gully
. 
333 rockfall events, 48 volcanic earthquakes, and four pyroclastic density currents (PDCs)

## TYPHOONS, CYCLONES, AND TROPICAL STORMS

### INVEST 95W
**Type:** Tropical disturbance with high cyclone development potential  
**Location:** West Pacific Ocean near Philippines  
**Date:** March 9-15, 2026  
**Severity:** 
INVEST 95W has a high likelihood of developing into a tropical cyclone, and is expected to recurve and likely exit PAGASA Monitoring Domain during 9–15 March 2026
  
**Impact:** 
A small increase in chance of very heavy rainfall for the northern tip of Myanmar; and a small increase in chance of extreme hot conditions is predicted for parts of southern Philippines, southern Sulawesi, and northern Papua
  
**Current Status:** 
INVEST 95W over the west Pacific Ocean is forecast to move slowly northeastward over the next two days
  
**Source:** PAGASA, Joint Typhoon Warning Centre (JTWC)

## SUMMARY NOTES

**Overall Regional Impact:** During the March 9-16, 2026 period, Southeast Asia experienced multiple concurrent natural disasters, primarily concentrated in Indonesia, the Philippines, Thailand, and Vietnam. The region faced significant flooding events, elevated volcanic activity at multiple sites, moderate seismic activity, and the potential development of a tropical cyclone.

**Climate Context:** 
La Niña conditions are predicted to weaken in February 2026 and transition to ENSO-neutral conditions in March 2026
, which has influenced the wetter-than-average conditions across the region.

**Most Affected Countries:** Indonesia and the Philippines were the most severely impacted, with Indonesia experiencing the highest casualties from flooding and landslides, while the Philippines dealt with ongoing volcanic eruptions at three major volcanoes and moderate earthquake activity.
