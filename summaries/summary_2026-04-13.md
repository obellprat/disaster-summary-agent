## **EARTHQUAKES AND AFTERSHOCKS**

### North Maluku Earthquake - Indonesia
**Type:** Major earthquake with tsunami  
**Location:** 
Molucca Sea, between North Sulawesi and North Maluku Provinces, Indonesia; epicenter near Batang Dua Islands at depth of 35 km
  
**Date:** 
April 2, 2026
  
**Severity/Magnitude:** 
Magnitude Mw 7.3 (BMKG) to Mww 7.4 (USGS)
  
**Impact:** 
One person killed, four injured, over 450 structures damaged or destroyed, majority in Batang Dua Islands
. 
Tsunami waves observed: 75 cm in North Minahasa Regency, 65 cm in Kema, and 5 cm at Davao City, Philippines
. 
In Manado: one death from sports centre collapse, two injured, Siloam Hospital seriously damaged, five government buildings and a hotel damaged; in Batang Dua Islands: one injured, 1,966 displaced, 104 homes collapsed
  
**Aftershocks:** 
At least 921 aftershocks, largest magnitude Mwb 6.2
  
**Current Status:** Response operations ongoing  
**Source:** Wikipedia (2026 North Maluku earthquake), USGS, Reuters

## **TYPHOONS, CYCLONES, AND TROPICAL STORMS**

### Typhoon Sinlaku (International Name)
**Type:** Typhoon intensifying to Super Typhoon  
**Location:** 
As of April 12 (8 PM), 2,680 kilometers east of Eastern Visayas
; 
Formed 234 nautical miles west-southwest of Chuuk
  
**Date:** 
Formed April 8, 2026; became typhoon April 10-11, 2026
; 
Developed into super typhoon April 12, 2026
  
**Severity/Magnitude:** 
Maximum sustained winds of 195 kph near center, gustiness up to 240 kph
; 
Forecast to reach Category 4-equivalent with 145-mph sustained winds and 175-mph gusts
  
**Movement:** 
Moving north-northwestward at 15 kph
  
**Impact:** 
Still outside Philippine Area of Responsibility; may enter PAR late next week with local name "Caloy"; low likelihood of landfall in Philippines
. 
Forecast to split the difference between Guam and Rota as Category 4-equivalent late Tuesday evening (April 15)
  
**Current Status:** 
U.S. bases on Guam in Condition of Readiness 3; typhoon watch in effect for Guam, Rota, Tinian and Saipan
  
**Source:** PAGASA, GMA News, Stars and Stripes, AccuWeather, Manila Times

## **VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY**

### Mount Kanlaon - Philippines
**Type:** Explosive volcanic eruption  
**Location:** Negros Island Region, Philippines  
**Date:** 
April 8, 2026, 5:51 AM to 6:47 AM
  
**Severity/Magnitude:** 
Explosive eruption producing 4,000 metres (13,000 feet) high "voluminous" plume drifting southwest with pyroclastic flows
  
**Impact:** 
Debris ignited vegetation causing wildfires near crater, contained April 9 after heavy rains
. 
More than 8,600 families (about 28,272 people) from 34 barangays displaced
. 
Classes suspended in La Carlota and La Castellana
  
**Current Status:** Alert Level 2; 
14 volcanic earthquakes recorded April 7; moderate ash plumes up to 300 metres
  
**Source:** Wikipedia (2024-2026 Kanlaon eruptions)

### Mount Taal - Philippines

### Mount Mayon - Philippines
**Type:** Effusive eruption with lava flows  
**Location:** Albay, Philippines  
**Date:** Ongoing as of April 6, 2026  
**Severity/Magnitude:** 
Ongoing effusive eruption with slow outflow of lava
  
**Impact:** 
Short-lived lava fountaining; sustained lava flows: 3.8 km in Basud Gully, 3.2 km in Bonga Gully, 1.3 km in Mi-isi Gully

344 rockfall events, 159 volcanic earthquakes, 12 pyroclastic density current signals
  
**Current Status:** 
Alert Level 3; six-kilometer permanent danger zone entry strictly prohibited
  
**Source:** Manila Bulletin, PHIVOLCS
