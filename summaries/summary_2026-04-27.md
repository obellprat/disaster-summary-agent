**Super Typhoon Sinlaku**
- **Location:** Northern Mariana Islands (not Southeast Asia proper, but monitored in region)
- **Date:** April 8-20, 2026 (peak impact April 14-15)
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Super typhoon status reached April 12
; 
Maximum sustained winds of 185 miles per hour and minimum central pressure of 896 millibars, making it one of the few category 5 storms to form between January and April

### VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY

**Philippines - Mount Kanlaon**
- **Location:** Negros Island Region, Philippines
- **Date:** Ongoing throughout April 20-27, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Alert Level 2 (scale 0-5); 
Continuing eruptive activity during April 14-22 with 12-53 daily volcanic earthquakes, including 1-7 daily periods of tremor lasting three minutes to just over one hour; sulfur dioxide emissions ranged from 680 to 2,313 tonnes per day

- **Impact:** 
Emissions generally rose 350-1,100 m above the summit; 1-5 periods of ash emissions almost daily
; 
Ash emissions on April 14 and 22 rose 600 m and 500 m above summit, drifted southwest

- **Current Status:** 
Alert Level remained at 2; public warned to stay out of 4-km-radius Permanent Danger Zone

- **Source:** The Watchers/GVP Weekly Volcanic Activity Report April 16-22, 2026

**Philippines - Mount Bulusan**
- **Location:** Philippines
- **Date:** April 22, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Increased seismicity with total of 101 volcano-tectonic earthquakes recorded during 0450-1330 on April 22; VT earthquakes associated with rock fracturing at depths of 2.5 km or less beneath the north edifice

- **Current Status:** Monitoring ongoing
- **Source:** The Watchers/PHIVOLCS

**Philippines - Mount Mayon**
- **Location:** Albay, Philippines
- **Date:** Ongoing throughout April 20-27, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Alert Level 3 (scale 0-5); 
Continuing eruption during April 8-14 characterized by lava effusion, pyroclastic density currents, incandescent rockfalls, ash plumes, and occasional minor Strombolian activity; lava flows remained 1.3, 3.8, and 3.2 km long in Mi-isi, Basud, and Bonga drainages

- **Impact:** 
By April 14, total of 4,020 people (1,105 families) staying in 12 evacuation shelters, and additional 85 people (26 families) staying with friends or relatives

- **Current Status:** 
Alert Level remained at 3; residents reminded to stay away from 6-km-radius Permanent Danger Zone

- **Source:** Global Volcanism Program

**Philippines - Taal Volcano**
- **Location:** Batangas, Philippines
- **Date:** April 6, 2026 (within broader reporting period)
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Very weak phreatic eruption observed at 3:08 a.m. on April 6; event generated plume that rose 200 meters above crater lake
