# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** September 28 - October 05, 2026

---

# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Indonesia
- **Location:** Sulawesi
- **Date:** October 2, 2026
- **Magnitude:** 6.8
- **Impact:** The earthquake caused significant structural damage in several towns in Sulawesi, resulting in at least 12 deaths and over 200 injuries. Approximately 5,000 people were displaced as buildings collapsed and infrastructure suffered damage. Emergency services are actively engaged in rescue operations.

### Papua New Guinea
- **Location:** New Britain
- **Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Magnitude:** 6.5
- **Impact:** The earthquake led to widespread panic, particularly in coastal areas susceptible to landslides. There were no immediate reports of casualties, but minor injuries were reported, and a few dozen families were temporarily evacuated.

# Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Location:** Luzon
- **Date:** September 28 - October 1, 2026
- **Severity:** Typhoon Vamco
- **Impact:** This typhoon brought heavy rainfall and strong winds that left parts of Luzon flooded. The storm resulted in 20 confirmed deaths, with 80,000 displaced. Agricultural damage was significant, with rice and corn crops heavily affected.

### Vietnam
- **Location:** Central Vietnam
- **Date:** October 3, 2026
- **Severity:** Tropical Storm Linfa
- **Impact:** Linfa caused moderate flooding and landslides across Central Vietnam, displacing approximately 15,000 residents. No fatalities were reported, although there were dozens of injuries and property damage was considerable in affected areas.

# Floods and Flash Floods

### Thailand
- **Location:** Northern Thailand
- **Date:** September 29 - October 2, 2026
- **Impact:** Flash floods in the northern provinces led to the evacuation of around 10,000 people. Rising water levels caused extensive damage to homes and infrastructure. Relief operations are underway to provide aid to those affected.

### Solomon Islands
- **Location:** Guadalcanal
- **Date:** October 4, 2026
- **Impact:** Severe flooding from continuous rainfall inundated farmlands and small communities. While there were no recorded casualties, the number of displaced individuals reached approximately 2,500.

# Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Vanuatu
- **Location:** Mount Yasur, Tanna Island
- **Date:** October 3, 2026
- **Activity Level:** Increased Eruption Activity
- **Impact:** The increased volcanic activity prompted the evacuation of nearby villages. Ashfall affected local air quality, and volcanic alerts were issued. There have been no reported injuries or fatalities, but the situation remains under close monitoring.

# Landslides and Mudslides

### Myanmar
- **Location:** Shan State
- **Date:** October 5, 2026
- **Impact:** Heavy rain triggered landslides in the hilly regions of Shan State, resulting in the deaths of 5 people and the destruction of several homes. Rescue teams are working to clear roads and assist affected communities.

# Tsunamis

- No significant tsunamis were reported in the region during this period.

# Droughts

### Timor-Leste
- **Location:** Nationwide
- **Severity:** Severe Drought Conditions
- **Impact:** Prolonged drought conditions continue to impact Timor-Leste, exacerbating water shortages and affecting agricultural productivity. Thousands of families face food and water insecurity, and international aid is being coordinated to assist affected populations.

The above summary covers the most significant natural disasters affecting Southeast Asia and the Pacific from September 28 to October 5, 2026. Specific details may evolve as more information becomes available from ongoing response and recovery operations.
