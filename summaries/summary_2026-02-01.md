- **Type:** Garbage/waste landslide
- **Location:** Barangay Binaliw, Cebu City, Philippines
- **Date:** January 8, 2026 (incident occurred before the reporting period, but recovery efforts continued through January 25-February 1)
- **Severity:** Major disaster
- **Impact:** 
  - 
36 people dead and 18 others injured

  - 
The incident involved mostly sanitation workers who were working in the site

  - Recovery operations completed by January 18, 2026
  - 
State of calamity declared in Cebu City on January 13

Oversaturation of landfill waste due to continuous rainfall over the previous weeks, combined with geotechnical lapses such as the stability of waste stockpiles, slope conditions, and landfill benching issues with landfill waste reaching a height of 35 m (115 ft) on a steep slope

- **Current Status:** Recovery operations concluded; investigations ongoing by government agencies
