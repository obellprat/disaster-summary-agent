### **TYPHOONS, CYCLONES, AND TROPICAL STORMS**

**Philippines - Tropical Storm Penha (Basyang)**
- **Type:** Tropical Storm
- **Location:** Philippines - Mindanao and Visayas regions
- **Date:** 
Made first landfall over Bayabas, Surigao del Sur on February 5, 2026; additional landfalls in Jagna and Dauis in Bohol, Alcoy, Cebu, and Ayungon, Negros Oriental on February 6

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Maximum sustained winds of 65-75 km/h, gustiness up to 90 km/h

- **Impact:** 
A total of 832,986 people were affected, while 60,384 were displaced across Calabarzon, the Bicol Region, Eastern Visayas, Negros Island, and Caraga
; 
₱43.14 million (US$733,086.58) in assistance provided
; 
2,850 passengers and 792 rolling cargoes stranded

- **Current Status:** 
System downgraded to remnant low on February 7, 2026, and dissipated

- **Source:** Wikipedia, Gulf News, Rappler, PAGASA

### **VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY**

**Philippines - Mount Kanlaon**
- **Type:** Volcanic eruption
- **Location:** Negros Island Region, Philippines
- **Date:** 
Moderately explosive eruption occurred on February 19, 2026, at 4:38 p.m., lasting two minutes

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Generated an eruption plume that rose to 2,000 m (6,600 ft) high and drifted southwest
; 
Alert Level 2 (moderate unrest)

- **Impact:** 
Pyroclastic flows observed descending the southern and southwestern slopes
; 
sulfurous fumes observed in nearby barangays; shock wave heard as booming sound
; 
Entry into 4-km radius permanent danger zone prohibited; local government units advised to prepare for possible evacuation

- **Current Status:** 
Remains under Alert Level 2 as of February 20; continued signs of unrest
; 
10 volcanic earthquakes and two episodes of tremor recorded in 24-hour monitoring period

- **Source:** Manila Times, Manila Bulletin, PHIVOLCS

**Philippines - Mount Mayon**
- **Type:** Volcanic eruption
- **Location:** Albay Province, Philippines
- **Date:** Ongoing through February 11-18, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Characterized by lava effusion and collapses at the summit dome, pyroclastic density currents, incandescent rockfalls, ash plumes, and active lava flows

- **Impact:** Ongoing evacuations and monitoring
- **Current Status:** Continuing eruption with elevated alert status
- **Source:** PHIVOLCS, Global Volcanism Program

**Indonesia - Mount Semeru**
- **Type:** Volcanic eruptions (multiple)
- **Location:** East Java, Indonesia
- **Date:** 
Erupted on February 19, 2026, at 4:00 PM WIB; in the past week, Mount Semeru erupted 40 times

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Continuing eruption with 87-90 seismically detected explosions reported daily (February 19-20); ash and gas plumes rose to a maximum of 800-1,000 m above the vent
; 
Activity level at Level III (Alert); throughout 2026, Mount Semeru has had 360 eruptions, the most in Indonesia

- **Impact:** 
People barred from activities within 5-km radius of crater; residents told to avoid areas within 500 meters of riverbanks along Besuk Kobokan due to potential hot clouds and lava flows reaching up to 17 km from crater; authorities urged vigilance for pyroclastic flows, lava avalanches and lahars

- **Current Status:** 
Alert level remained at "Level 3 - Standby" (on a 4-level scale)

- **Source:** VolcanoDiscovery, PVMBG, Databoks, ANTARA News

**Indonesia - Mount Ibu**
- **Type:** Volcanic activity
- **Location:** Halmahera, North Maluku, Indonesia
- **Date:** February 12-18, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Activity continued with daily eruptive events recorded, except on February 17; white-to-gray or gray ash plumes rose 300-600 m above the summit

- **Impact:** 
Public advised to stay 2 km away from active crater and 3.5 km away from the N crater wall opening

### **LANDSLIDES AND MUDSLIDES**

**Indonesia - Central Aceh Landslide**

- **Location:** Pondok Balik, Ketol District, Central Aceh Regency, Indonesia
- **Date:** 
Ongoing as of February 16, 2026

- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
More than 18 rai (roughly three hectares) of land have given way, damaging roads and nearby farms

- **Impact:** 
Moving earth is now only about 400 meters away from residential houses and advancing at nearly one meter per day due to persistent heavy rainfall and weakened soil conditions
; 
power lines relocated as precaution
; 
evacuation remains a possibility

- **Current Status:** 
Local officials on constant monitoring

- **Source:** Diskurso PH

**Indonesia - Morowali Nickel Hub Landslide**
- **Type:** Landslide/tailings failure
- **Location:** Morowali, Sulawesi, Indonesia
- **Date:** February 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 
Second major tailings failure at the same facility within 11 months; in March 2025, a landslide buried four workers

Suspected to have been caused by soft soils indicating water saturation as primary contributing factor

- **Source:** Discovery Alert Australia

---

**Summary:** The period of February 15-22, 2026, saw Southeast Asia affected primarily by volcanic eruptions, earthquakes, and the aftermath of Tropical Storm Penha. The most significant events included Mount Kanlaon's explosive eruption on February 19, ongoing eruptions at Mount Semeru and Mount Mayon in the Philippines, routine seismic activity across the Philippines and Indonesia, and a slow-moving landslide threatening communities in Central Aceh, Indonesia. Tropical Storm Penha (Basyang), which made landfall on February 5-6, continued to impact communities during the early part of this period with flooding and displacement affecting over 832,000 people.
