# Natural Disasters in Southeast Asia and the Pacific (August 3-10, 2026)

## Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Indonesia
- **Location**: Sulawesi
- **Date**: August 6, 2026
- **Severity**: Magnitude 6.8
- **Impact**: Moderate structural damage reported in Palu. Casualties include 25 confirmed deaths and over 100 injuries. Approximately 5,000 people were displaced.
- **Current Status**: Recovery efforts underway with national aid being distributed.

### Philippines
- **Location**: Mindanao
- **Date**: August 9, 2026
- **Severity**: Magnitude 5.9
- **Impact**: Limited damage in Cotabato City. Mostly minor injuries reported with few structural damages.
- **Current Status**: Normalcy returning as aftershocks decrease.

## Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Typhoon Name**: Typhoon Mirinae
- **Location**: Northern Luzon
- **Date**: August 4-6, 2026
- **Severity**: Category 4
- **Impact**: Significant wind damage and flooding in Cagayan Valley and Ilocos Region. Around 50 fatalities and 200,000 displaced. Widespread power outages and infrastructure damages.
- **Current Status**: Relief operations in progress with international aid arriving.

## Floods and Flash Floods

### Vietnam
- **Location**: Mekong Delta
- **Date**: August 3, 2026
- **Severity**: Severe flooding due to prolonged rains
- **Impact**: Widespread crop destruction and inundation of village homes. An estimated 15,000 people affected with 5 missing.
- **Current Status**: Water levels decreasing as rains subside; relief efforts accelerating.

### Thailand
- **Location**: Bangkok
- **Date**: August 7, 2026
- **Severity**: Flash floods
- **Impact**: Disruption in transportation and commerce. Minimal casualties but considerable economic impact in urban areas.
- **Current Status**: Floodwaters receding; city services being restored.

## Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Papua New Guinea
- **Volcano Name**: Mount Ulawun
- **Location**: New Britain
- **Date**: Eruption on August 5, 2026
- **Severity**: Major eruption
- **Impact**: Ashfall affecting surrounding communities. 3,000 residents evacuated, no casualties reported but significant disruption to air travel.
- **Current Status**: Continuous monitoring, secondary eruptions possible.

## Landslides and Mudslides

### Indonesia
- **Location**: Central Java
- **Date**: August 8, 2026
- **Severity**: Triggered by heavy rains
- **Impact**: Landslide buried a village, resulting in 20 fatalities and several missing. Around 100 displaced residents.
- **Current Status**: Search and rescue operations ongoing.
