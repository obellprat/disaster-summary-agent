# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** August 17 - August 24, 2026

---

# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

## Indonesia
- **Location**: Sumatra, Indonesia
- **Date**: August 20, 2026
- **Magnitude**: 6.8
- **Impact**: The earthquake resulted in 12 casualties and over 300 individuals injured. Infrastructure damage included collapsed buildings and disrupted transportation routes in the affected area. Thousands of people were displaced, with ongoing relief efforts to provide shelter and medical aid.
- **Current Status**: Emergency services are conducting rescue operations and damage assessments. Aftershocks have been mild but persistent.

# Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

## Philippines
- **Location**: Northern Luzon, Philippines
- **Date**: August 18-21, 2026
- **Name**: Typhoon Maring
- **Severity**: Category 4
- **Impact**: Typhoon Maring struck with winds of up to 150 mph, causing widespread flooding and landslides. Casualties totaled 48 with hundreds injured, and approx. 10,000 people displaced. Significant agricultural and infrastructure damages occurred, especially in rural areas.
- **Current Status**: The typhoon has moved northward out to sea. Relief efforts are concentrated on providing food, water, and temporary shelters to the affected populations.

## Vietnam
- **Location**: Central Vietnam
- **Date**: August 22-24, 2026
- **Name**: Tropical Storm Liem
- **Severity**: Tropical Storm
- **Impact**: Although downgraded from a typhoon, it caused severe flooding in coastal regions, leading to 5 fatalities and hundreds evacuated. The storm damaged roads and power lines, disrupting the local economy.
- **Current Status**: The storm has dissipated. Cleanup operations are underway, with ongoing efforts to restore power and access to isolated communities.

# Floods and Flash Floods

## Thailand
- **Location**: Northeastern Thailand
- **Date**: August 19, 2026
- **Severity**: Severe flooding
- **Impact**: Heavy rains caused rivers to overflow, leading to flash floods and affecting thousands of households. Approximately 30,000 people were displaced, and transport networks were severely disrupted.
- **Current Status**: Floodwaters are receding, and recovery efforts are being initiated to restore homes and infrastructure. Temporary shelters are housing affected populations.

# Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

## Papua New Guinea
- **Location**: Rabaul, East New Britain Province
- **Date**: August 23, 2026
- **Activity**: Moderate volcanic eruption
- **Impact**: The eruption of Tavurvur Volcano spewed ash plumes disrupting air travel and affecting nearby communities with ashfall. No casualties were reported, but a precautionary evacuation of surrounding areas was enacted.
- **Current Status**: The eruption has ceased, but monitoring continues with a watch for potential further activity. Evacuees are awaiting clearance to return home.

# Landslides and Mudslides

## Malaysia
- **Location**: Sabah, Malaysia
- **Date**: August 20, 2026
- **Impact**: Heavy rainfall triggered landslides in hilly areas, resulting in 4 fatalities and several injuries. Road access was blocked in some rural areas, complicating rescue efforts.
- **Current Status**: Landslide sites are being cleared, and the government is providing assistance to affected communities. Roads are being repaired to restore connectivity.

# Tsunamis

No significant tsunami events were reported in the region during the specified period.

# Droughts

## Timor-Leste
- **Location**: Nationwide, Timor-Leste
- **Date**: Ongoing since early August 2026
- **Severity**: Severe
- **Impact**: Prolonged lack of rainfall has led to water shortages affecting agriculture and rural communities. Around 100,000 people are facing food insecurity due to crop failures.
- **Current Status**: The government is coordinating with international organizations to provide food aid and improve water access. Long-term solutions are being explored to mitigate future drought impacts.
