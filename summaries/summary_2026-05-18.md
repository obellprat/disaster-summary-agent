## FLOODS AND FLASH FLOODS

### Flooding - South Sumatra, Indonesia
**Type:** Flooding  
**Location:** Musi Rawas Utara Regency (South Sumatra), Indonesia - Karang Jaya, Rupit, Karang Dapo, and Rawas Ilir districts  
**Date:** May 7, 2026 at 0400H UTC+7  
**Severity/Magnitude:** Major flooding across 4 districts  
**Impact:** 
As of 10 May 2026 at 2045H UTC+7, one fatality was reported, while approximately 14.2K households (54.3K people) were affected. Around 14.2K houses were affected, including approximately 35 houses heavily damaged, 11 moderately damaged, and 101 slightly damaged. Reported damages also included five bridges, 16 health facilities, 44 educational facilities, 35 places of worship, 11 inundated office buildings, 27 inundated fishponds, 30 hectares of rice fields, and 850 hectares of plantations
.  
**Current Status:** 
The Regent of Musi Rawas Utara declared an Emergency Response Status for Hydrometeorological Disaster for the period of 7–20 May 2026
. 
As of the latest update, floodwaters in Karang Jaya District had receded, water levels in Rupit District had started to decrease, floodwaters from Kerta Sari Village to Karang Dapo in Karang Dapo District had begun to recede although Biaro Village remained inundated, Rawas Ilir District was still flooded
.  
**Source:** BNPB, AHA Centre

## VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY

### Mayon Volcano - Philippines
**Type:** Volcanic eruption with pyroclastic density currents  
**Location:** Albay Province, Bicol Region, Philippines  
**Date:** Ongoing activity including May 2, May 13, and throughout May 11-18  
**Severity/Magnitude:** Alert Level 3; pyroclastic density currents, lava flows, ashfall  
**Impact:** 
At least 124 barangays across Albay Province have been affected by ashfall, lava flows, pyroclastic density currents, and volcanic activity. More than 287,000 people across the Philippines remain displaced or are sheltering due to the prolonged eruption. Evacuation centers across Albay Province continue housing thousands of families
.  
**Current Status:** 
As of May 15, 2026, Mayon Volcano is actively erupting in Albay Province
. Activity continuing with lava flows, rockfalls, and pyroclastic events.  
**Source:** PHIVOLCS, IQAir

### Mount Semeru - Indonesia
**Type:** Continuing volcanic eruption  
**Location:** East Java, Indonesia (border of Lumajang and Malang regencies)
