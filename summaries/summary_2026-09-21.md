# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

## Earthquake in Indonesia
- **Location:** Sulawesi, Indonesia
- **Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Severity:** Magnitude 6.7
- **Impact:** 
  - Casualties: 12 fatalities, over 150 injured
  - Displaced people: Approximately 5,000 evacuated
  - Damage: Significant structural damage in several towns, with roads and bridges heavily impacted
- **Current Status:** Ongoing aftershocks, emergency response teams deployed
