# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

## Indonesia
- **Location:** North Maluku
- **Date:** November 5, 2026
- **Severity:** Magnitude 6.8
- **Impact:** Minor structural damage reported in nearby towns. No casualties. People temporarily evacuated from coastal areas in fear of tsunamis.

# Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

## Philippines
- **Location:** Eastern Visayas
- **Date:** November 3-5, 2026
- **Severity:** Typhoon (Category 4)
- **Impact:** 12 fatalities, over 30,000 displaced, severe flooding in low-lying areas, significant agricultural loss.
- **Current Status:** Relief operations underway. Power and communication lines being restored. International aid requested.

## Vietnam
- **Location:** Central Vietnam
- **Date:** November 3-6, 2026
- **Severity:** Tropical Storm
- **Impact:** Widespread flooding, 5 fatalities, thousands evacuated. Crops and infrastructure damaged.
- **Current Status:** Floodwaters are receding. Rescue operations continue, and government assistance is being distributed.

# Floods and Flash Floods

## Thailand
- **Location:** Southern Thailand
- **Date:** November 4-9, 2026
- **Severity:** Heavy monsoon rains
- **Impact:** Severe flooding displacing approximately 10,000 people, 3 fatalities, houses and roads damaged.
- **Current Status:** The military is assisting with evacuations and relief distribution. Weather conditions improving.

## Papua New Guinea
- **Location:** East Sepik Province
- **Date:** November 2-7, 2026
- **Severity:** River overflow due to continuous rain
- **Impact:** 4,000 people displaced, flooding of agricultural land, and minor landslides reported.
- **Current Status:** Efforts are focused on providing temporary shelters and food supply to affected communities.

# Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

## Indonesia
- **Location:** Mount Merapi, Central Java
- **Date:** November 7, 2026
- **Severity:** Increased volcanic activity, minor eruption
- **Impact:** Ash plume affecting nearby villages, precautionary evacuation of 5,000 residents.
- **Current Status:** Monitoring continues, residents advised to stay alert for further evacuations.

# Landslides and Mudslides

## Myanmar

- **Severity:** Heavy rain-induced landslide
- **Impact:** Blocked roads, 15 houses buried, 7 fatalities reported, rescue operations in progress.
