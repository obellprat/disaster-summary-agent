## **VOLCANIC ERUPTIONS AND ACTIVITY**

**Philippines - Kanlaon Volcano**
- **Date:** March 15, 2026
- **Location:** Negros Island
- **Type:** Moderately explosive eruption
- **Details:** 
A "moderately explosive" eruption occurred at Kanlaon Volcano on Negros Island on Sunday evening at 06:07 p.m., generating a two-kilometer-high ash column currently drifting to the southwest

- **Severity:** 
This is the third such event at Kanlaon Volcano so far this year

- **Status:** Alert Level 2 remains in effect
- **Source:** GMA Network, PHIVOLCS

**Philippines - Mayon Volcano**
- **Date:** Ongoing through March 12-18, 2026
- **Location:** Albay Province, Luzon
- **Type:** Continuing eruption
- **Details:** 
The eruption at Mayon continued during 12-18 March, characterized by lava effusion and collapses at the summit dome, pyroclastic density currents (PDCs), incandescent rockfalls, ash plumes, active lava flows, and occasional minor Strombolian activity

- **Severity:** Alert Level 3 (Increased Tendency Towards Hazardous Eruption)
- **Impact:** 
Lava flow lengths remained at 3.8 km in the Basud Gully (E flank), 3.1 km in the Bonga Gully (SE flank), and 1.3 km in the Mi-isi Gully (S flank)

- **Source:** Global Volcanism Program, PHIVOLCS

**Philippines - Bulusan Volcano**
- **Date:** March 22, 2026
- **Location:** Sorsogon Province
- **Type:** Alert Level raised
- **Details:** 
The alert level of Sorsogon province's Bulusan Volcano was raised from 0 to 1 on Sunday, March 22, as the volcano "entered a period of low-level unrest" and has recorded 475 volcanic earthquakes at Bulusan since March 15

- **Status:** Alert Level 1 (low-level unrest)
- **Source:** Rappler, PHIVOLCS

**Indonesia - Mount Lewotobi Laki-laki**
- **Date:** March 19, 2026
- **Location:** East Nusa Tenggara
- **Type:** Eruption
- **Details:** 
Mount Lewotobi Laki-laki erupted on Thursday (March 19, 2026) at 14:36 WITA with the observed ash column height around 1,000 meters above the peak or 2,584 meters above sea level

- **Severity:** 
The ash column was observed to be gray in color with thick intensity towards the north and northeast

- **Status:** Level II (Caution)
- **Source:** MAGMA Indonesia, PVMBG

**Indonesia - Mount Semeru**
- **Date:** Ongoing through March 16-23, 2026 (including March 22)
- **Location:** East Java
- **Type:** Continuing eruption
- **Details:** 
Mount Semeru erupted again on Sunday (March 22, 2026) at 07:41 WIB and has erupted 53 times in the last week

- **Severity:** 
Activity continued at Semeru during 12-18 March, with daily eruptive events recorded by the seismic network

- **Status:** Level III (Alert/Standby)
- **Source:** PVMBG, Global Volcanism Program

**Indonesia - Mount Ibu**
- **Date:** March 20, 2026 (ongoing)
- **Location:** North Maluku
- **Type:** Continuing eruption
- **Details:** 
Mount Ibu in North Maluku erupted again on March 20, 2026, with 24 eruptions in the past week
; 
Activity at Ibu continued during 12-18 March, with daily eruptive events recorded by the seismic network. White-and-gray ash plumes rose 300-700 m above the summit and drifted N, NE, E, and SE on most days

- **Status:** Alert Level 2
- **Source:** PVMBG, Global Volcanism Program
