# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** June 08 - June 15, 2026

---

## Earthquakes and Aftershocks

### Indonesia
- **Location:** Sulawesi
- **Date:** June 12, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** 6.5
- **Impact:** Moderate structural damage in several towns, 10 casualties, and hundreds displaced.
- **Current Status:** Recovery efforts are ongoing with international aid arriving.

## Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

### Philippines
- **Location:** Luzon and Visayas
- **Date:** June 9, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Category 4 Typhoon
- **Impact:** Severe flooding and wind damage, approximately 150,000 people displaced, 25 confirmed deaths, significant damage to infrastructure and agriculture.
- **Current Status:** Relief operations are in progress; roads are being cleared for aid delivery.

### Solomon Islands
- **Location:** Guadalcanal
- **Date:** June 13, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Tropical Storm
- **Impact:** Heavy rainfall and high winds caused local flooding; minor infrastructure damage and no casualties reported.
- **Current Status:** Weather conditions are improving; local communities are engaged in clean-up operations.

## Floods and Flash Floods

### Vietnam
- **Location:** Mekong Delta
- **Date:** June 10-11, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Continuous heavy rains led to severe flooding
- **Impact:** Widespread flooding affected approximately 40,000 residents, with 5 fatalities and significant agricultural losses reported.
- **Current Status:** Water levels are starting to subside with relief efforts underway to supply food and shelter.

## Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

### Papua New Guinea
- **Location:** Rabaul Caldera
- **Date:** June 14, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Moderate eruption
- **Impact:** Ash plume reached nearby towns, causing minor respiratory issues and temporary displacement of 2,000 people.
- **Current Status:** The situation is stable; monitoring continues, and local evacuation orders have been lifted.

## Landslides and Mudslides

### Myanmar
- **Location:** Chin State
- **Date:** June 8, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Heavy rains caused landslides
- **Impact:** 3 villages affected, 15 casualties, and landslide debris blocked roads, impeding transportation.
- **Current Status:** Clearing operations are in progress; temporary shelters provided for affected families.

## Tsunamis

- No significant tsunami events reported during this period.

## Droughts

### Kiribati
- **Location:** Nationwide
- **Date:** Ongoing as of June 15, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Severe
- **Impact:** Water scarcity affecting approximately 60,000 residents, with a significant impact on agriculture and public health.
- **Current Status:** International aid being coordinated to supply water and support water-saving initiatives.
