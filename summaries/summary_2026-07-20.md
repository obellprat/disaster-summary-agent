# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** July 13 - July 20, 2026

---

### Earthquakes and Aftershocks

**Location:** Papua New Guinea  
**Date:** July 15, 2026  
**Severity/Magnitude:** Magnitude 6.7  
**Impact:** The earthquake struck the eastern part of Papua New Guinea, causing structural damage to several buildings and displacing around 2,000 people. Fortunately, no casualties were reported. Emergency services were promptly deployed to provide assistance to the affected regions.  
**Current Status:** Emergency response ongoing, with efforts focused on providing temporary shelters and assessing further structural risks.

### Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

**Location:** Philippines  
**Date:** July 17-19, 2026  
**Severity:** Typhoon Category 4  
**Impact:** The typhoon made landfall in the northern region of Luzon, affecting approximately 1.2 million people. It resulted in significant infrastructure damage, including power outages in several provinces. There were reports of at least 25 casualties and thousands displaced. The government has begun coordinated relief efforts, focusing on restoring power and providing essential supplies.  
**Current Status:** Clean-up operations are underway, and restoration of utilities is a priority.

**Location:** Solomon Islands  
**Date:** July 18, 2026  
**Severity:** Tropical Storm  
**Impact:** This storm caused heavy rainfall and moderate flooding in coastal areas, damaging homes and roads. There have been no reports of casualties, but around 500 people were evacuated as a precautionary measure.  
**Current Status:** The government is working on repairing infrastructure and assessing potential threats from subsequent storms.

### Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

**Location:** Indonesia (Mt. Merapi)  
**Date:** July 14, 2026  
**Severity:** Moderate eruption  
**Impact:** Mt. Merapi released an ash plume 5 kilometers into the sky, impacting air travel and communities within a 10-kilometer radius. There were no injuries reported, but local authorities evacuated approximately 3,000 people as a precaution.  
**Current Status:** Volcanic activity has decreased but is still being closely monitored by the Geological Agency.

### Floods and Flash Floods

**Location:** Vietnam (Central Highlands)  
**Date:** July 16, 2026  
**Severity:** Severe flooding due to torrential rains  
**Impact:** The intense flooding affected over 200,000 residents, causing widespread property damage and disrupting transport links. At least 10 people lost their lives, and thousands were evacuated to safer areas. Response teams are assisting with evacuation efforts and providing emergency relief supplies.  
**Current Status:** Water levels are steadily receding, and relief efforts are expanding to restore access to isolated communities.

### Landslides and Mudslides

**Location:** Myanmar (Shan State)  
**Date:** July 14, 2026  
**Severity:** Severe landslide  
**Impact:** The landslide, triggered by heavy monsoon rains, resulted in the destruction of numerous homes and at least 15 casualties. Efforts are focused on search and rescue operations, and temporary shelters have been established for the displaced populace.  
**Current Status:** Ongoing rescue operations with support from government and international organizations.

### Droughts

**Location:** Nauru  
**Date:** Ongoing as of July 2026  
**Severity:** Severe drought conditions  
**Impact:** The persistent drought has led to critical water shortages, impacting agriculture and daily life for the island's inhabitants. The government has declared a state of emergency, and international support is being requested to provide potable water supplies.  
**Current Status:** Efforts are being made to import water and enhance desalination capacities.

These summaries capture the significant natural disasters occurring in Southeast Asia and the Pacific from July 13 to July 20, 2026, highlighting the regional impacts and ongoing recovery efforts.
