# SDC Hub Bangkok: Disasters in Southeast Asia & the Pacific
**Report Period:** May 25 - June 01, 2026

---

# Earthquakes and Aftershocks

## Indonesia
- **Location:** Sumatra, Indonesia
- **Date:** May 27, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Earthquake, Magnitude 6.5
- **Impact:** The earthquake resulted in significant structural damages in the region, displacing approximately 1,200 people. There were 23 reported casualties and over 100 injuries.
- **Current Status:** Emergency services were deployed, and temporary shelters were established for the displaced population.

## Papua New Guinea
- **Location:** Bougainville, Papua New Guinea
- **Date:** May 30, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Earthquake, Magnitude 5.8
- **Impact:** The earthquake caused moderate infrastructure damage and resulted in two casualties. Several communities reported disrupted utilities.
- **Current Status:** Recovery efforts are ongoing, with repairs to power and water supplies a priority.

# Typhoons, Cyclones, and Tropical Storms

## Philippines
- **Location:** Luzon, Philippines
- **Date:** May 26-28, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Typhoon "Maring," Category 4
- **Impact:** Widespread flooding and wind damage affected over 500,000 people. At least 37 deaths were reported, with thousands displaced due to the intense storm surges.
- **Current Status:** Relief operations are underway, focusing on delivering food, clean water, and medical services to affected areas.

## Vanuatu
- **Location:** Port Vila and surrounding areas, Vanuatu
- **Date:** May 29-31, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Cyclone "Kevin," Category 3
- **Impact:** High winds and heavy rains caused considerable damage to infrastructure and crops. Several remote villages were isolated due to landslides and communications outages.
- **Current Status:** Assessment teams are working to determine the extent of the damage and coordinate an international response.

# Floods and Flash Floods

## Vietnam
- **Location:** Hanoi and Northern Vietnam
- **Date:** May 25-27, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Severe flooding following heavy rainfall
- **Impact:** Over 20,000 homes were inundated, causing mass evacuations. Five fatalities were confirmed, with several bridges and roads washed out.
- **Current Status:** Water levels have begun to recede, allowing for initial clean-up and reconstruction efforts to commence.

# Volcanic Eruptions and Activity

## Indonesia
- **Location:** Mount Sinabung, North Sumatra, Indonesia
- **Date:** June 1, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Eruption, high ash emissions
- **Impact:** The eruption forced the evacuation of nearby villages due to the threat of pyroclastic flows and ashfall. No casualties were reported, but agricultural activities are severely affected.
- **Current Status:** Continuing volcanic activity is being closely monitored, with exclusion zones enforced.

# Landslides and Mudslides

## Myanmar
- **Location:** Chin State, Myanmar
- **Date:** May 28, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Large landslide following heavy rain
- **Impact:** The landslide destroyed several homes, resulting in 11 confirmed deaths and multiple missing. Access to affected regions is limited due to debris.
- **Current Status:** Rescue operations are ongoing, with additional efforts aimed at clearing and stabilizing the affected slopes.

# Tsunamis

No significant tsunami events were reported in the region during this period.

# Droughts

## Marshall Islands
- **Location:** Entire Marshall Islands
- **Date:** Ongoing as of June 1, 2026
- **Severity/Magnitude:** Severe drought conditions
- **Impact:** The drought continues to affect water supplies and agriculture, with thousands experiencing water shortages. The government has declared a state of emergency.
- **Current Status:** International assistance is being mobilized to provide water and drought-resistant resources to the affected communities.
